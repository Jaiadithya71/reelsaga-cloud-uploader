## Scope and method

Tightened/thresholded subtitle-band OCR was used after clothing caused noisy OCR. Suspected wording defects were checked in actual pixels and short audio segments. This was a full-duration transcript-to-caption review, not continuous normal-speed viewing or frame-perfect certification.

Also inspected 48 full-frame samples. Automated large scene-change scan found 5-15 changes per approximately 37-49 second part. All 24 passed a near-freeze scan for intervals of at least three seconds, with no detected events. Long intervals between detected large cuts include E9P1 (27 seconds), E11P3 (24 seconds), E3P2 (23.5 seconds). These are not static-image durations: action/camera movement continues, and subtle cuts can be missed.
